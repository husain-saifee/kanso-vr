# Kanso VR — Tone of Voice & Copy Guide

**Status:** Approved Reference  
**Audience:** Patients, Clinical Therapists, Researchers  
**Core Purpose:** Ensure all textual and spoken communication fosters calm, dignity, autonomy, and psychological safety.

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Calm & Plain Language:** Use simple, grounded words. Speak directly to the person in second person ("you"), never clinical jargon ("patient", "subject", "specimen").
2. **Non-Judgmental & Non-Shaming:** There are no failures, wrong choices, or underperformance. If a user stops, looks away, or exits, that is self-regulation to be respected.
3. **No False Reassurance or Medical Claims:** Never say "You will be completely safe" or "This reduces anxiety." Simply state what the environment is doing and what choices the user has.
4. **No Time Pressure or Urgency:** Avoid countdowns, exclamation marks, or words like "Quickly", "Hurry", or "Respond now".
5. **No Shouting:** Never use all-caps headers or button text. Use Sentence case or clean Title Case.

---

## 2. Terminology Mapping & Banned Words

| Avoid (Do Not Use) | Use Instead | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `CLAUSTROPHOBHIA` | `Enclosed Space (Claustrophobia)` | Fix typo; lead with descriptive clarity. |
| `AQUAPHOBIA` | `Rising Water (Aquaphobia)` | Clear plain-language framing. |
| `ACROPHOBIA` | `High Elevation (Acrophobia)` | Clear plain-language framing. |
| `PERSON STATUS` | `Session History` | Eliminates cold, dehumanizing phrasing. |
| `STILL FOCUS TIME` | `Gaze Focus Duration` | Plain language explanation. |
| `WATER RISE RATE` | `Water Rise Speed` | Familiar terminology. |
| `WALL MOVE DURATION(sec)` | `Movement Duration` | Human-readable units and formatting. |
| `Time Elasped:` | `Elapsed Time` | Correct spelling; standard capitalization. |
| `MAXIMUM ALTITUDE REACHED!` | `Summit reached. You may pause or continue.` | Non-startling, informative milestone. |
| `SESSION COMPLETE!` | `Session completed gently.` | Calm closure instead of arcade fanfare. |
| `Exiting in 3... 2... 1...` | `Returning to menu...` | Removes countdown anxiety. |
| `Wrong` / Red X's | Helpful guidance | Never shame the user. |
| `Reduces anxiety` | `Supports gradual exposure` | Zero unsupported efficacy claims. |

## 3. Standard Strings Reference

### 3.1 Main Menu
- Welcome Title: *"DePhobia"*
- Subtitle: *"A safe, self-paced virtual reality space for confronting fear."*
- Primary Navigation:
  - *"Begin Session"*
  - *"Settings & Comfort"*
  - *"Session History"*
  - *"Exit Experience"*
- Phobia Selectors:
  - *"Enclosed Room"* — *"Practicing calm and breathing in an enclosed space."*
  - *"Rising Tide"* — *"Gentle exposure to coastal water levels."*
  - *"Open Heights"* — *"Practicing comfort at elevating altitudes above a city."*

### 3.2 In-Session Pause & Emergency
- Title: *"Session Paused"*
- Subtitle: *"Take your time. You are always in control."*
- Options:
  - *"Resume Session"*
  - *"Recenter View"*
  - *"Save & Return to Menu"*
- In-World Emergency Beacon Label:
  - *"Emergency Exit"*
  - Subtext: *"Look here or tap to return to menu at any time."*

### 3.3 Start & End Rituals
- Start Ritual:
  - *"Find a comfortable seated or standing position."*
  - *"Take a deep, gentle breath."*
  - *"When you feel ready, select Begin."*
- End Ritual:
  - *"Session complete. Take a quiet moment to breathe."*
  - *"You may return to the menu whenever you are ready."*
