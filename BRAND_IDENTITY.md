## 1. Etymology & Philosophical Foundation

### 1.1 The Meaning of Kanso (簡素)
*Kanso* is one of the seven foundational Zen aesthetic principles (*Wabi-sabi*, *Shibui*, *Kanso*, *Shizen*, *Yugen*, *Datsuzoku*, *Seijaku*). It embodies:
> **Simplicity through the elimination of the non-essential.** It is not the absence of thought, but the refinement of thought until only that which serves calm, clarity, and truth remains.

### 1.2 Evolution from "DePhobia"
| Dimension | Legacy Prototype ("DePhobia") | Modern Experience ("Kanso VR") |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Psychological Metaphor** | Combative ("de-phobia", fighting/eradicating fear) | Acceptance & Adaptation (finding equilibrium within space) |
| **Aesthetic Tone** | Harsh primary colors, startle car horns, flashing alerts | Soft twilight horizons, harmonic 432 Hz pads, gentle breathing rings |
| **Patient Agency** | Trapped in forced countdowns, punished with red text | In full control: voluntary start, always-accessible exit, peaceful summit |
| **Audience Perception** | Diagnostic, clinical, potentially stigmatizing | Mindful, dignified, medical-grade, serene |

## 2. Core Brand Attributes

- **Spacious:** Creates emotional and visual breathing room. Never claustrophobic or cluttered.
- **Composed:** Slows autonomic arousal. Every interaction is measured, quiet, and predictable.
- **Trustworthy:** Complete transparency. Zero hidden telemetry, zero startle stimuli, zero surprise movements.
- **Unburdened:** Strips away arcade scoring, timers, and performative metrics.
- **Accessible:** Conforms to WCAG 2.2 AAA standards so every human can participate safely.

## 3. Brand Identity & Visual Geometry

### 3.1 The Brand Mark: *The Horizon Ensō*
The visual mark combines the Japanese *Ensō* (an open hand-drawn circle representing acceptance and presence) intersecting with an infinite, flat *Horizon Line* representing grounding and equilibrium.

```
                  . - ~ ~ ~ - .
              . '               ' .
            /                       \
           /                         \
    ───────┼─────────────────────────┼───────  [ The Horizon ]
           \                         /
            \                       /
              . _               _ .
                  ' - , _ , - '
```

- **Open Circle (Ensō):** Open-ended, non-judgmental, accepting of human distress without pathology.
- **Horizon Axis:** Absolute vestibular grounding and physical stability in VR.

## 5. Typography Standards

- **Primary Typeface:** TextMeshPro SDF with geometric sans-serif (Liberation Sans SDF / Inter / Open Sans).
- **Scale:**
  - `Display / Logo`: 32pt (Semi-Bold)
  - `Headers`: 24pt (Medium)
  - `Body Copy`: 16–18pt (Regular, line-height 1.35)
  - `Captions`: 14pt (Regular, tracking +0.02)
- **Formatting Rule:** Title Case for interactive controls; Sentence case for body prose. **All-caps shouting is banned.**

---

## 6. Sound & Motion Signature

- **Acoustic Signature:** Pure algorithmic sine/fifth chord tuned to 432 Hz with continuous cosine swell envelope. Zero transients, zero click seams.
- **Haptic Signature:** Informational micro-pulses (40–80ms, 15–25% amplitude) delivering tactile certainty without startle.
- **Motion Preset (`breathe`):** 4.5s ease-in-out cosine curve mirroring healthy human respiration.
